## English (en-US)

**App name** (max 30 characters)
Doost – Persian Poetry Library

**Short description** (max 80 characters)
237 Persian poets, 133,000 poems, verse search and Faal-e Hafez, offline.

**Full description**
Doost is a pocket library of Persian poetry: the works of 237 poets and writers, from Hafez, Saadi, Rumi and Ferdowsi to modern poets, with more than 133,000 poems and three million verses, complete and fully offline. Poems are typeset beautifully in the traditional couplet layout; search poet names, poem titles and the text of every verse, undisturbed by half-spaces or diacritics, and narrow a search to a single poet or book. Faal-e Hafez, the cherished tradition of seeking guidance from Hafez's Divan, is presented with calm respect: form your intention, open the Divan, and read the ghazal with its interpretation. Bookmark poems, continue where you left off, share a single verse or a whole poem as text or as an image, and tap any word to look up its meaning in the Yaad dictionary. Doost collects no personal data and shows no ads.
